**A pack raffle on Robinhood Chain.** Buying QPULL pays a 4% tax and mints sealed packs. 80% of the tax becomes QUOTRON
prizes, paid out by a daily raffle, a weekly leaderboard and a weekly draw for Art Pass holders. Every random number
is a Drand beacon, verified onchain.
